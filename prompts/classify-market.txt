How to classify market_relevance:

CRITICAL — event vs. analysis:
"high" is reserved for EVENT news — something specific happened on or near the publication date that a trader would react to. Analysis pieces, trend reports, "hidden debts", "state of the sector", roundups of known corporate facts, editorial commentary, and think pieces are NOT high, no matter how many named companies appear. They are, at best, "medium".

- "high" ONLY IF ALL of the following hold:
  1. A specific event happened on or near the publication date (rate decision, earnings report, M&A announcement, IPO listing, guidance change, sanction, lawsuit filing, regulatory ruling, product launch).
  2. The event is causally traceable to a specific ticker or a tight sector proxy — a trader could name what they'd trade and why.
  3. The story is REPORTING that event, not analysing a longer-term trend that happens to name it.

- "medium" IF the story is market-relevant but is analysis, commentary, a trend piece, or a policy shift with diffuse impact — no single-session trigger, or the trigger is too broad to isolate.

- "low" IF the story mentions a market entity in passing but the impact is marginal (a colour piece on a CEO, a culture story that involves a company).

- "none" IF there is no meaningful market angle: sports, entertainment, human interest, opinion, ordinary crime, ceremonial events, natural disasters with no policy or commodity impact, individual political scandal without policy consequence.

Rules for tickers:
- At most 3 tickers per story. Prefer 1-2 tight, obvious ones over 3 stretched ones.
- Every symbol MUST be from the whitelist. If nothing fits, return [].
- If market_relevance is "low" or "none", tickers MUST be [].
- Prefer the tightest layer: a single named company > a sector ETF > a country/region ETF > a broad index.
- Do NOT stretch: if the connection to a ticker would need two hops of reasoning, leave it out.

Multi-subject rule (IMPORTANT):
If the story names ≥3 companies with roughly equal billing (a "big tech" piece, "top pharma names", "the FAANGs", a sector-wide roundup), DO NOT sample two of them and pretend they represent the story. Pick the SECTOR or INDEX ETF instead — QQQ / XLK for big tech, XLV for pharma, XLF for banks, XLE for energy, etc. Individual tickers only when the story is genuinely about one or two named companies.

Regional fallback (IMPORTANT):
When the story is clearly about a specific listed company that is NOT in the whitelist (e.g. an Indian bank, a Chinese A-share manufacturer, a Brazilian miner), fall back to the country/region ETF that best represents it. This is not "stretching" — it is the sensible proxy so the reader can still see how that market moved. Prefer:

Only apply this fallback when relevance is "high" or "medium" AND the story is clearly about a specific company or a domestic market event. Do not attach a country ETF to a story that only tangentially mentions the country.
